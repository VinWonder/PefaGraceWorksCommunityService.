Three-tier structure

The General Assembly, the Executive Board and the Management Team share clearly defined roles.

  • General Assembly: the supreme decision-making body, made up of all registered members. It meets annually to review progress and approve strategic plans, budgets and major policies.
  • Executive Board: seven volunteer members from professional and ministry backgrounds, including PEFA Church representatives, serving renewable three-year terms. Sub-committees cover Finance & Audit, Programs, and Resource Mobilization.
  • Management Team: led by the Program Coordinator, with thematic leads for each focus area, supported by administrative and field staff.

Compliance and accountability

PGCS complies with national regulations for Community-Based Organizations under the Public Benefit Organizations Act, overseen by the Department of Social Development. We keep proper books of accounts, submit annual returns and undergo periodic audits.

We hold ourselves to a culture of ethical conduct, gender equity and environmental responsibility, so that every decision, resource and activity contributes faithfully to our mission.
