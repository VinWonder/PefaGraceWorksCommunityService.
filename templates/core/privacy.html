What we collect

We collect only what we need: your name and contact details when you contact us, volunteer, donate, register for an event or give feedback; and household information when you ask for help, so that we can assess needs fairly.

How we use it

To respond to you, process and receipt donations, plan programs, report to partners in summary form (never with names unless you agree), and meet our legal obligations to the Department of Social Development.

Who can see it

Only authorised PGCS staff and volunteers who need it for their role. We do not sell or share your data. M-Pesa payments are processed by Safaricom.

How long we keep it

As long as needed for the purpose you gave it, and for financial records as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, by contacting us at {{ site.email|default:site.phone }}.

Photos

We publish photos of people only with their consent, and of children only with a parent or guardian's consent.
